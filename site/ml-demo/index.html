    Noteheads by staff and voice slot

    On this page nearly every notehead landed in one voice slot. The model reads stem direction poorly where two voices share a stem (common in older hymnals); the app will need its own voice logic for such pages.

    Voice slot 1 is the stems-up voice on a staff (soprano on the treble, tenor on the bass); slot 2 is stems down (alto, bass). Staves are found from the detected clefs.

    Families
