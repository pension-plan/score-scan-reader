Score Scan Reader: optional ML symbol detector
==============================================

copista2m-dense.int8w.onnx, copista2m-rel.int8w.onnx, copista2m-classes.json

  These files are derived from the copista-2m model by KobiMusic (https://kobi.music),
  https://huggingface.co/kobimusic/copista-2m (revision c37d89e1e52edc95c1a73bda0aea5f2f71a9c763),
  licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (LICENSE.copista-Apache-2.0.txt).
  The detector's page pipeline (src/ml-worker.js) follows KobiMusic's copisteria reader,
  https://github.com/kobimusic/copisteria (Apache-2.0).
  Modified: exported from PyTorch to ONNX as two graphs (dense trunk, relational verifier),
  weights re-encoded as per-channel int8 with DequantizeLinear (fp32 compute). See
  NOTICE.copista-2m and NOTICE.copisteria, which are kept as section 4(d) of the licence asks.
